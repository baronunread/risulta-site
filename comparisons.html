Risulta optimizes for a very small self-hosted footprint. That makes it a strong fit for some websites—and the wrong tool for others.

Choose Risulta when…

  • You want analytics on one small VPS with minimal moving parts.
  • Simple traffic metrics are more useful than a large reporting suite.
  • You want multiple websites, one login, and locally controlled data.
  • You are comfortable operating backups, updates, and uptime.

Choose something else when…

  • You need funnels, goals, ecommerce revenue, geolocation, or campaign tooling today.
  • You need a managed service with support and no server responsibility.
  • Your traffic requires distributed storage or high-availability ingestion.
  • You need deep advertising attribution or integrations with an ad platform.

Primary sources: Plausible self-hosting, Umami installation, and Google Analytics data collection. Verify current vendor documentation before making a production decision.
