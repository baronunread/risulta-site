What comes next.

Last updated August 24, 2026. Completed work and current priorities are tracked publicly.

Available now

  • Authentication and multi-site isolation ShippedOne administrator login can manage multiple websites, users, and site-scoped viewers.
  • Single-server deployment ShippedOne binary, SQLite storage, systemd service, health check, Caddy configuration, and release CI.

Production durability

  • Online backups and restore drills NextSafe SQLite snapshots with a documented, tested recovery path.
  • Schema migrations NextVersioned migrations with fixtures for every public release.
  • Account recovery and session controls NextPassword changes, administrator recovery, and session revocation.
  • Operational visibility NextStructured logs, basic counters, trusted-proxy boundaries, and graceful overload behavior.

Sustained analytics performance

  • Published VPS benchmarksRepeatable ingest, latency, memory, disk-growth, and dashboard measurements.
  • Rollups and retentionHourly and daily summaries with bounded raw-event retention.
  • Dashboard cachingFaster summaries without weakening website isolation.

Funnel analysis, GeoIP databases, realtime websockets, organizations, and external analytics databases remain deliberate non-goals for now.
